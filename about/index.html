About Raphael

A scientist shaped
by three continents.

PhD candidate in Medicinal Chemistry and mentor.

UNIVERSITY OF MICHIGAN

Education and
research training.

My scientific path began in pharmacy at the University of Nigeria, where an undergraduate project on HIV-1 protease introduced me to structure-based computational drug discovery.

At Taipei Medical University, I trained in medicinal chemistry, organic synthesis, and molecular simulation while developing celecoxib-based histone deacetylase inhibitors for glioblastoma. I completed the degree as a Taiwan Government Scholar.

A predoctoral internship at Academia Sinica expanded my computational work into peptide self-assembly. I am now completing doctoral training in Medicinal Chemistry at the University of Michigan.
